Student Success Score: Mathematical Formulation & Scoring Logic

A formal technical explanation of how multi-dimensional institutional indicators are clamped, weighted, normalized, and protected by fail-safe hard floors with zero black-box obscurity.

1. Composite Success Score Formulation

The overall Student Success Score \(S \in [0, 100]\) is calculated as a strictly bounded linear combination of 6 orthogonal domain sub-indices:

S = 0.25 · I_academic + 0.20 · I_attendance + 0.20 · I_placement + 0.15 · I_lms + 0.10 · I_engagement + 0.10 · I_skills
Constraint: \(\sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i = 1.0000000000\) (Cryptographically verified at boot: c4520cb10d79)

Sub-Index Mathematical Definitions

Dimension Weight Mathematical Formula Variables & Raw Data Sources Normalization & Bounds
Academic Index (\(I_{\text{acad}}\)) 25% clamp(CGPA × 10 - 3 × min(backlogs, 5), 0, 100) CGPA (0-10), Active Backlog Count from SIS / ERP Clamped to \([0, 100]\) with 3-point backlog deduction
Attendance Index (\(I_{\text{att}}\)) 20% clamp(Mean_Att - 5 × count(sub_att < 60%), 0, 100) Overall % + Subject-wise attendance from RFID / Biometric Clamped to \([0, 100]\) with 5-point penalty per critical subject
Placement Readiness (\(I_{\text{place}}\)) 20% 0.35 × Aptitude + 0.40 × Coding + 0.25 × MockInterview Aptitude assessments, DSA code editor runs, Mock interview scores Weighted sum of verified technical competencies
LMS Activity (\(I_{\text{lms}}\)) 15% 0.50 × AssignmentCompletion% + 0.50 × ScaledLogins Moodle/Canvas submissions, Active daily learning sessions Normalized log of weekly interactions & submission rates
Engagement (\(I_{\text{eng}}\)) 10% min(100, (Events + Clubs + 2×Hackathons + 2×Certs)/20 × 100) Hackathon participation, Tech clubs, Verified certificates Linear scaling up to institutional cap of 20 weighted credits
Skills Inventory (\(I_{\text{skills}}\)) 10% 0.60 × TechSkillScore + 0.40 × SoftSkillScore Technical stack assessments + Communication evaluations Holistic industry competency synthesis

2. Fail-Safe Hard-Floor Safety Rules (Zero Black-Box Guarantees)

In high-stakes higher education, composite weighted averages alone can mask severe localized danger (e.g., a student with 90% coding score who is about to be debarred due to 48% attendance). Hard-floor rules override composite scoring to ensure immediate intervention.

🚨 Attendance Hard Floor
Attendance < 60.0%
Overrides tier to Priority Support and pins student to top of advisor queue to prevent examination debarment.
🚨 Backlog Hard Floor
Active Backlogs ≥ 2
Overrides tier to Priority Support regardless of high marks in other subjects to ensure academic recovery.
🚨 CGPA Hard Floor
CGPA < 5.0
Triggers immediate escalation for remedial subject tutoring and core conceptual mentoring.

3. Advisor Work Queue Priority Ranking Algorithm

Faculty advisors have limited hours each week. CampusPulse orders the advisor queue using a deterministic priority score \(P\) that weights deficit severity, negative momentum, and hard-floor flags:

P = (100 - S) + 50 · \mathbb{I}_{\text{HardFloor}} + 15 · \mathbb{I}_{\text{Momentum}=\text{DOWN}} + 10 · \mathbb{I}_{\text{OverdueIntervention}}
Result: Ensures advisors always see the highest-urgency students first without arbitrary subjective guesswork.
